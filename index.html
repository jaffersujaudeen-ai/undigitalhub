Delivery expectations

Standard services are targeted for delivery within 1–3 days after requirements are complete, the quotation is approved, and agreed initial payment conditions are satisfied. Complex projects, missing information, approval delays and third-party dependencies may need a different, confirmed timeline.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price range I see the final price?

No. It is an indicative starting range. Your final price depends on scope, content, complexity and the number of pages or slides, and is confirmed in a quotation before work begins.

Can I see a sample before I order?

Yes. Every service page includes a labelled sample so you can judge the quality before committing to anything.

How do I pay?

Payment terms — full advance, deposit plus balance, or milestone-based — are agreed in your quotation and recorded against your order.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Each quotation states a revision limit. Work beyond that scope is quoted separately before it begins.

What if my project does not fit the standard categories?

Message us with what you have in mind. We can put together a custom quotation for more complex work.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you are trying to create and we will guide you to the right service and a fair quotation.
